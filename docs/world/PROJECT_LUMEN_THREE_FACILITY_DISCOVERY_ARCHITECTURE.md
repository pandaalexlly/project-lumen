No final geography, scene, script, or unbriefed-player result is defined here.** This document reconciles [Spatial Topology Design](PROJECT_LUMEN_SPATIAL_TOPOLOGY_DESIGN.md), [Complete Player Journey](PROJECT_LUMEN_COMPLETE_PLAYER_JOURNEY.md), [Endgame Knowledge Reassembly](PROJECT_LUMEN_ENDGAME_KNOWLEDGE_REASSEMBLY.md), [Atrium Cognitive Hub Design](PROJECT_LUMEN_ATRIUM_COGNITIVE_HUB_DESIGN.md), and [Knowledge Architecture V2](PROJECT_LUMEN_KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE_V2.md). ## Ontology before mystery: three perspectives, not three world states

“Original,” “duplicated,” and “deep/core” are designer-facing names for **materially connected research perspectives within one fixed facility system**. They are not three menu destinations, three runtime copies of a map, or three whole-building quantum configurations. Fixed ground carries the Atrium H0, circulation, P/Q receiver beds, survey/service references, exterior ground, and the evacuation shaft. Only specifically bounded quantum objects participate in relocation; the currently proposed room-scale pair is M and N. An ordinary down-only lift visits *different already-built* lower landings. Its descent does not select a world state.

The shared player error is to use one familiar address, plan, or room name as if it named one enduring **material object**. The present-day correction can be proved without establishing the historical cause of near-duplication. K8—how original, duplicated, and core accounts belong to one persistent history—is still a **provisional inference** until continuous fixed cross-site evidence and an object-specific comparison are spatially demonstrated. Do not invent an incident mechanism, designate one shell “real” and the other “fake,” or claim the entire facility was quantum merely to make a dramatic document reveal.

| Layer of truth | What physical play can establish | What remains an historical question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stable world rule** | Observation constrains a bounded member/current or candidate placement; the present object keeps identity and attached condition; fixed infrastructure stays fixed. | Who first discovered, concealed, or misunderstood the law. |
| **Spatial relationship** | M and N can be distinguished simultaneously and can exchange fixed P/Q addresses; their ordinary connections change with occupation. | Whether near-matching construction arose by design, accident, or some earlier event not presently evidenced. |
| **Three-site interpretation** | A continuous fixed reference and the same identifiable shell/condition, if eventually made inspectable from multiple site viewpoints, could connect apparently conflicting accounts. | The full chronology and human responsibility; neither papers nor today's exchange alone can prove it. |

The sites should therefore give **different honest observations of the same system**, not one answer per facility. Each can contain known rules, misleading but reasonable local interpretations, and evidence that only acquires meaning after a later physical experiment. Documents add provenance and human consequence; they never supply the sole operative rule or authorize a route.

## Original operations facility — a dependable baseline with unreliable names

1. **First interpretation.** Awakening and the upper H0 Atrium make this look like a conventional abandoned research workplace containing an anomalous Cube. Its plant, services, Records, Power, Containment, and Signal work routes seem stable. A P-side frontage can plausibly be damaged or shut, while the Cube/Beam apparatus is the exception.
2. **Actual role in the planned truth.** This is the fixed operational frame against which every later claim can be checked. Staff routinely dispatched work by *address*, an efficient practice when the occupant seemed stable but an unreliable way to name a bounded room shell. Their observations were often accurate at the time. The site does not need to have been ignorant, staged, or false; the player repeats its reasonable address-based mistake.
3. **Knowledge strengthened.** Completed Awakening establishes K1/K3 without being reclassified as a trick. A genuinely vacant A/B/C candidate may clarify K2; Containment can test K4; fixed contacts versus attached apparatus can support K6; a known-member lens comparison can support K5. Repeated H0/P visits prepare K7, but a single changed frontage is not its proof. K8 cannot be inferred simply from an operations label.
4. **Environmental storytelling.** A partly restored central plant, fixed receiver wear, conduits that visibly serve equipment, interrupted survey work, and credible damage convey a facility used by people. Common work orders and shift notes refer to P because staff worked by location. Power notes may blame real supply faults; Containment handling accounts may call the collar a machine; Signal logs may treat footage as surveillance history. These are locally plausible, not planted lies. The player can compare the actual contact, repair, and lens path without reading any of them.
5. **Discovery order.** The player must encounter the upper site first through the completed Awakening-to-H0 flow, but can investigate its four work approaches in many orders. A Records-first visitor sees contradictory histories before knowing rooms can move; a Containment-first visitor expands the object boundary; a Signal-first visitor may notice a lens or Q without knowing its significance. Each can return to the same H0 datum. Clues do not appear only after a wing is completed.
6. **Reasonable false reading.** “The experiments are abnormal; the building is not.” Later, a changed P face can be blamed on repair, navigation error, power, or one altered door. This belief should fail only when both persistent M/N identities and fixed P/Q references are compared before and after a lawful empty exchange. Do not have a note announce that the Atrium wall is quantum.
7. **True-ending relevance.** Original-site fixed ground supplies H0, the P contact and approach, ordinary access for comparison and blind release, and at least one prospective view of the fixed spur X. The player can learn the observation method here, then recognize that `N@P` is a *current occupant*, not a permanently sealed address. None of the four work areas is a credential for M@P; the relevant physical evidence must remain available to an early insightful player.

The original site is thus more than a starting zone. Its unchanged H0 plant and P scar become the player's control sample after every unsettling visit. If it becomes unrecognizable between returns, later “same place, different occupant” reasoning has no stable basis.

## Duplicated room/work facility — two conserved histories, not a copied level

1. **First interpretation.** A near-matching work branch and two similar room fronts can look like standardized construction, a second wing, a remodeled version of one room, a teleport destination, or a loaded copy of H0. From Q or exterior ground, the player may see a room-like structure without yet knowing whether it is the same one seen at P.
2. **Actual role in the planned truth.** M and N are **two materially present bounded room shells** with distinct post-construction wear and ordinary attached features. Neither is spawned when a player enters. They may reciprocally exchange fixed P/Q sockets while the Atrium and outer service frame remain still. “Duplicated” describes near-matched architecture and conflicting work histories, **not** a whole alternative facility that replaces the original at runtime. The exact historical origin of the resemblance is not settled by current design evidence.
3. **Knowledge strengthened.** K6 becomes architectural: M's integrated repair, existing doorway, and present ordinary condition follow M, while P/Q contacts do not. K7 requires the player to identify **both** shells, observe a reciprocal *empty* exchange, and check fixed ground. K1/K3 still constrain participating walls; K5 may later explain a real lens hold if its line is visible. K2 is not proven by the binary occupied P/Q pair. K8 may gain a material comparison but remains unproven without cross-site continuity.
4. **Environmental storytelling.** M's repair and doorway relationship, N's different wound and solid face, socket-specific wear, survey supports, and mismatched fixed contacts are the primary evidence. Ordinary repair material and work accounts belong where those workers operated. If a record physically travels with a shell, its holder must be an authored attached member; fixed service boards stay with their socket. Different staff can truthfully describe different occupants under the same address. Damage should show *ownership* through continuity across floor/wall/door joins, not through color-coded labels or loose props.
5. **Discovery order.** The player may glimpse one shell from H0, the other from Signal's fixed Q approach, or the exterior C perspective before recognizing the pair. They may read a survey inconsistency first, or see an exchange first and seek a record afterward. The decisive evidence remains available from fixed access without a required C ride, core descent, or dossier. An early lawful `M@P` connection must remain usable even if the player cannot yet explain the history.
6. **Reasonable false reading.** “One room was renovated,” “two ordinary copies were built,” “a lift moved a room,” or “the whole building changed.” The first mismatch does not refute these. A before-action prediction about **both** shells' repair/door or wound/solid-face packages, unchanged P/Q contacts, and the changed ordinary rear connection does. A watched-wall hold and repeatable release can challenge a random loading explanation. The word “teleport” alone is not failure if the player correctly tracks two bounded identities and fixed places.
7. **True-ending relevance.** M's already-existing doorway is the *material feature* that may meet X when M lawfully occupies P; N's solid face makes P seem sealed in the proposed initial `N@P, M@Q` occupation. Signal's Q view and the Atrium's P view must let the player compare that relationship before attempting a release. A camera can remove or provide a real observation constraint but cannot summon M to P. The route is an ordinary connection confirmed after inspecting both shells, not an exit spawned by understanding.

This branch should not monopolize “the revelation.” Power makes fixed versus attached state legible; Records supplies independent histories; Containment supplies the smaller boundary precedent; Signal supplies the other address and possible observer. The branch is where those interpretations meet, not a final answer room.

## Deep/core facility — a third fixed perspective, not a quantum lower state

1. **First interpretation.** After the lawful exterior C trip, a damaged down-only evacuation lift seems to carry the player to basement service floors. Repeated central architectural grammar at successive landings may instead feel like a loop or one Atrium losing exits. The player may expect a hidden “real explanation” at the bottom.
2. **Actual role in the planned truth.** L1–L4 are **different built places** along one continuous fixed shaft. Their four/three/one/zero lateral research approach pattern reflects different service purposes and histories, not an elevator-controlled facility transformation. The core provides foundational service/survey and genuine refuge/egress. It can reveal what earlier staff compared, feared, or misunderstood and may offer a third physical perspective on the same reference system; it is not an extra quantum root or a machine selecting spatial states. The specific event that produced near-matched room histories remains an open historical design question.
3. **Knowledge strengthened.** Distinct landings challenge “same plan = same material room,” preparing a more careful K7 identity question but **not proving quantum exchange**. Continuous shaft and service references could support K8 if a same-shell attached condition can be compared against independent fixed contacts across site viewpoints. K5 may reframe an old monitoring trace; K6 may reframe a stored setting versus a disconnected supply. K1–K4 do not acquire new rules downstairs.
4. **Environmental storytelling.** Built approach counts, shaft depth/joints, and landing-specific integrated repairs are the first proof that these are different fixed places. Ordinary service wear and refuge provision give them credible human use. Optional fragments can echo upper survey addresses, contact behavior, handling repairs, and camera coverage, one incomplete viewpoint at a time. They should connect previous physical evidence and human cost without saying “the rooms exchanged” or prescribing M@P. A fragment can be missed without making the physical route illegible.
5. **Discovery order.** Core follows a voluntary, visible commitment to the ordinary lift from C. The player can reach C and investigate Q/X before boarding, or take the lift and reach real safety. A first-playthrough true route can be found **without visiting core**. A player who does descend may carry new questions into a later playthrough, but the game does not require a Bad Ending, replay state, or collected fragments before it permits the upper route.
6. **Reasonable false reading.** “The elevator selects progressively emptier quantum facility states,” “H0 is looping,” or “these are merely ordinary floors and have nothing to do with the upper mystery.” The continuous shaft, unique repairs, and built—not shuttered—differences reject the first two. The last is literally compatible with Model C but may leave the intended *interpretive* comparison underpowered; optional physical references and human traces should prompt a question about why upper addresses were treated as stable, not assert a new lift mechanic.
7. **True-ending relevance.** The core can make the upper M@P deduction emotionally and historically richer, but supplies no required key, switch, document, or unique rule. Its fixed reference might eventually corroborate K8; it cannot be the only place a player learns that M has a doorway, that P/X are adjacent, or that a lens can hold a shell. A player who chose real evacuation made a valid escape, not a mechanically failed attempt.

The **down-only commitment is an epistemic constraint**. If L4 is reached with no ordinary return, a K8 proof that requires physically revisiting H0 afterward cannot be mandatory on that same run. Do not invent a hidden return shortcut or a replay flag to conceal this. Either the necessary cross-site comparison is prospectively available before commitment, or the core's contribution remains optional retrospective corroboration; its exact geometry and proof route require a later design decision. This preserves the first-playthrough True Ending.

## How the three perspectives combine without one answer site

The player should be able to hold several plausible but incomplete accounts at once. The wrong hypotheses become productive when each predicts a different result that can be checked on **physical evidence**, not when one office explains the answer in text.

| Shared question | Original operations perspective | Duplicated-branch perspective | Deep/core perspective | Decisive physical comparison and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What did “the room at P” mean?** | Dispatch and supply name a fixed address. | Two shells have separate repairs/attached histories. | Repeated plans show familiar names need not imply material identity. | Both M and N at P/Q before and after empty reciprocal exchange, with fixed contacts unchanged. Lower plans alone cannot prove it. |
| **What moved and what kept its condition?** | Cube/Beam, A/B/C beds, and P contact supply a small and a fixed baseline. | Repairs/door condition travel with M or N; external connection changes. | Old service outputs may record the connection, not the object's internal state. | Change or inspect an attached condition, follow the same marked object, and compare the fixed old contact. No snapshot recall. |
| **Why did a lawful change not happen?** | Player/Beam reach and candidate views may still constrain a member. | A participating room wall can be watched; current/candidate poses matter. | Monitoring infrastructure can explain staff's incomplete accounts. | Same-powered live lens on/blocked-member contrast on a known object precedes any consequential Q hold; footage alone is historical evidence. |
| **Are these one connected history?** | H0, P, and original survey work provide one end of a fixed datum. | Q and shell-specific repair/condition provide a second independent viewpoint. | Shaft/core footing may provide a third fixed exposure and human account. | A continuous inspectable fixed reference **plus** the same identifiable shell/condition compared with independent socket traces could support K8. Its walkable/viewable geometry is not yet proved; three documents do not substitute. |

Information distribution follows work, not rarity. Common documents can be found on ordinary desks; misleading documents are honest local interpretations; contradiction documents sit by the instrument or damage that made their authors question a result; later core fragments deepen the human account. None is a collectible gate. A player who ignores all paper must still be able to prove K1/K3/K5/K6/K7 where those laws affect the true physical route. K8's *historical why* may remain partly interpretive if the material cross-site proof cannot be authored; it must never secretly determine whether M@P works.

## Encounter paths and first-run agency

- **Object-first:** Awakening → Containment/Power's larger bounded structure and fixed contact → Signal's known-member camera contrast → H0/P and Q shell comparison. The lower core may never be visited before a first-run M@P crossing.
- **Architecture-first:** H0/P inconsistency → Records' two material histories → Signal/Q fixed view → repeatable empty M/N comparison. The player may later use Power/Containment to explain *how* a room could move, but no flag delays an early correct inference.
- **Exterior-first:** A lawful supported trip reaches C; from fixed perimeter ground the player can inspect a question about Q/X and return before boarding the down-only lift. They may pursue M@P on the same run or choose genuine evacuation through the distinct lower landings.
- **Evacuation/replay:** A player descends to real refuge with an incomplete upper model. Lower differences and optional fragments recontextualize earlier notes, contacts, cameras, and room names; a later playthrough uses the **same unchanged upper rules**. Replay offers new *player knowledge*, not New Game+ state or an eligibility token.

These are likely reasoning routes, not a chapter order. Viewpoints may be encountered early without their meaning being known; the game does not defer spawning a scar, live lens, or exterior spur until the player has read a particular page. An early first-playthrough True Ending remains possible even if the planned emotional sequence is short-circuited.

## Fairness and validation gates

1. **No whole-facility shortcut in the fiction.** Avoid the claims “the original building became quantum,” “the duplicated site loads in its place,” or “the elevator changes the world state.” If a future historical account requires one of those, it is a separate world-rule revision, not an implied result of M/N.
2. **Independent material evidence.** Before detailed sites are drawn, specify fixed H0↔Q/C/shaft reference exposures, the two shell histories, and what each site's workers could truly have seen. Keep viewable and walkable connections distinct. If a cross-site K8 comparison is not physically possible, label K8 contextual rather than mechanically proven.
3. **Access and early escape.** Essential M/N identity, P/Q fixed references, Q lens target if used, and X's relationship to P must be discoverable from ordinary upper/perimeter access before any irreversible core descent. No core-only document or lower landing may be required for M@P, and C survival remains valid.
4. **Causal human tests.** Use unbriefed prospective predictions: same marked object/current condition across sockets; camera clear versus blocked with power unchanged; both M/N identities at opposite fixed addresses; and the M@P/X connection before crossing. Record whether the player distinguishes historical inference from present physical proof. The [cognitive integration plan](PROJECT_LUMEN_CORE_MYSTERY_COGNITIVE_INTEGRATION.md) is a protocol, not a passed result.
5. **Narrative restraint.** Let repairs, camera paths, service continuity, ordinary documents, and differing built plans carry the inference. If players can only reconstruct the three-site relationship from a final terminal or all four fragments, revise evidence architecture. If they treat the core as an answer vault, its perspective has overwhelmed the physical question.

**Decision:** the three-site narrative can be structured as one mystery **without** adding a whole-facility quantum law or a replay gate. Present room exchange and true-route causality have bounded physical premises; the exact origin of the duplicated branch and full K8 cross-site proof remain unresolved. Do not promote those historical claims to final truth or build final geometry until the material comparison, one-way-access constraint, and unbriefed understanding are validated.
