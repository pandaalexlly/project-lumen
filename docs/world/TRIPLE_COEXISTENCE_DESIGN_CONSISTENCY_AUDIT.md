## 4. Deprecated assumptions and cognitive/evidence audit

**Do not carry these older premises into the triple-facility test:** one H0 with M/N at P/Q as the entire final geography; “original / duplicated / core” as merely three departments of one built facility; M/N exchange or a changing frontage by itself as proof of H0/H1/H2; a lower lift selecting a facility state; or four document fragments as the route to truth. The [rulebook](PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) supersedes the first two geographic premises. The [Bad Ending document](PROJECT_LUMEN_BAD_ENDING_AND_REPLAY_STRUCTURE.md) already rejects the latter two as physics/gate claims, although its inherited site layout still needs a separate reconciliation with the new map.

**Cognitive chain:** The active scenario and run sheet correctly seek `one changing place → contradictory remembered identities simultaneously present → revised map`. They also correctly keep early-correct players in the baseline distribution and refuse to credit an H0-return insight to viewpoint A/B. The chain is **not guaranteed by the test**: floor-level handoffs may read as ordinary transport or a load; different inboard/exterior repair meshes are matching motifs rather than a continuous physical joint; and an H2 sketch may provoke a comparison before the intended proof. These are explicit human-test risks, not documented passes. The final view is unnecessary as a *reversal* for a player who already inferred multiplicity; that result tests the lead-in, not the view.

**Evidence hierarchy:** The A/B/C [older evidence architecture](EVIDENCE_ARCHITECTURE_AND_MISINTERPRETATION_MAP.md) and the [M/N evidence contracts](PROJECT_LUMEN_EVIDENCE_CONTRACTS.md) correctly insist on observation, repeatable contradiction, and documents as corroboration. Their *specific* collar or two-socket experiments answer different questions. A Left/Right exchange could support “a bounded room can move” but would not establish that three fixed atria coexisted during earlier visits. Beam and camera can constrain a participating surface if their live line of sight is real; neither observes “the facility state” as a whole or substitutes for direct player observation of all three units. The triple prototype intentionally tests no Beam/camera rule. Bad Ending papers may motivate reinspection but cannot carry or gate the final proof.

**Prototype boundary:** The greybox leaves Left/Right/primed rooms out; the cognitive wrapper uses deterministic H0 → H1 → H2 → H0 player-position handoffs, floor-level receivers, mirrored *motifs* rather than one continuous repair mesh, fixed partitions, and a final H0 front-pocket arrival. None of these is a production QER destination law, one-way door solution, room-exchange simulation, or proof of the canonical horizontal topology. The technical validators establish fixed transforms, walkability, sampled occlusion/sightlines and safe handoffs, not exhaustive camera-angle coverage or comprehension. Keep the validator printouts and developer H labels hidden from participants.

**Testing chain:** The current run sheet, pilot checklist, analysis template, and session sheet largely agree on `T0` volunteered visits, `T1` H2 sketch, `T2` H0 return, `T3` locked first view, and `T4` diagnostic crossover; on `B1–B4`, `I/C/E/R`, spontaneous versus neutral elicitation, and on separating assigned from actual A/B exposure. They measure reasoning more carefully than a correct noun or count. D5–D8 are the specific places where a moderator or analyst could still produce incomparable data. No blank form or technical pass is a human result.

## 5. Open questions — do not invent answers in the pilot

1. Does the canonical three-unit map retain any production relationship to the older M/N/P/Q true-route design? No current document authorizes a one-to-one renaming or defines the full Left/Right exit/frontage table.
2. What exact observation is sufficient to say a player inferred **three facilities**, rather than three atriums or three ordinary wings? The current exposure threshold and full-score language do not fully settle the vertical-identity requirement.
3. Can the same unbriefed player form the intended one-changing-site belief after the *implemented floor-level* handoffs and chosen Awakening primer, without an explanation of QER travel? The pilots and scored baseline distribution must answer this empirically.
4. Can a moderator consistently direct A/B before any revealing view from the actual H0-return orientation? Do the two approaches yield comparable first-view exposure before crossover?
5. Which, if any, confidence prompt will be part of the locked scored protocol after the pilots? Until then, confidence comparisons are conditional.
6. Can the future production QER-only entry, room-to-atrium exit, and one-way threshold be authored under the rulebook without smuggling in the floor-level test handoff? This is a later production design question, not a reason to alter the isolated probe now.

## 6. Actions before player testing

1. **Before logistics pilots:** give moderators only the current cognitive run sheet, pilot checklist, and blank session sheet as operative materials. Label the older proof-only protocol and M/N geography as historical/reference on the internal briefing; do not read answer-bearing design files in front of participants. Record the exact build and one selected nonverbal primer.
2. **Run the two unscored pilots:** one assigned A-first and one B-first. Verify scene reset, H2 pause, audio/video, route instructions before reveal, adequate exposure, first-view lock, and whether confidence wording leads. Log any early proof leak or transfer-artifact interpretation. Pilot pass means **measurement procedure works**, not that the puzzle succeeds.
3. **Before scored recruitment:** publish a short protocol decision note resolving D5–D8: operative document precedence, primer, confidence prompt, route wording, and the predeclared vertical-stack criterion. If a pilot cannot produce comparable first-view records without prototype changes, stop and request a separate design decision; do not improvise mid-wave.
4. **Documentation follow-up, separately scoped:** mark D1–D4/D7 historical or update their status and cross-links. Keep every disagreement visible in the change record; do not silently rewrite old M/N, QER, or ending laws. No gameplay or geometry change follows automatically from this audit.
5. **After actual sessions:** use the analysis template's two denominators: baseline belief among all eligible participants and proof-view revision only among clean `B1` participants with no T2 correction and an adequate view. Preserve guesses, early-correct models, ordinary-wing accounts, and contaminated cases as distinct outcomes. Human comprehension stays **UNVALIDATED** until that analysis exists.

**Audit conclusion:** The isolated implementation is consistent with the *fixed-three-stack/no-exchange* test boundary. The highest pre-test risks are stale authority links and a still-ambiguous bridge between atrium-cue exposure and full-facility inference. Neither technical geometry validation nor aligned terminology in the forms establishes the intended human cognitive reversal.
