| Task | Deliverable | Completion condition |

| TASK-034 — Paper Exploration and Escape Test | Static diagrams for full current/candidate/arrival envelopes, support and fixed access; test the reduced finale alongside the prior sequence and A/B/C recall, including empty C and a consumed release opportunity | Unbriefed participants predict membership, support/payload cases and geometric observation; every recall has a real reobservation path and safe fixed access. Resolve source-support and mapped-camera rejection risks. No gameplay code, new scenes or silent perceptual-darkness variant |

TASK-034 is a decision gate. Record participants' actual explanations, incorrect predictions, hints needed and successful transfers of knowledge. Do not claim a human test occurred if only a desk review was possible. Small samples are diagnostic evidence, not proof of universal comprehension.

Do not start implementation merely because the design document is complete. If internal-observer exceptions, unexplained contact rules or mandatory solution text are needed, revise TASK-032/033. Evaluate the fallback ending explicitly rather than quietly adding another mechanic.

## Phase 4: Final mystery

| Task | Deliverable | Completion condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TASK-041 — Exterior Transfer Escape | The previously validated A/B/C assembly, visible exterior receiving evidence, selective observation setup and safe physical exit | The informed solution leaves exactly one legal alternative; every failed attempt and empty-assembly recall works; a lucky legal success is accepted; no final-only rule appears |
| TASK-042 — Narrative and Environmental Reinterpretation | A consistency pass over facility purpose, incident chronology, traces, ordinary equipment and the ending view | Earlier anomalies gain a coherent second meaning. The final revelation can be expressed by the player before any concluding exposition |
| TASK-043 — First Complete Mystery Playtest | End-to-end external sessions and a decision report; regression of preserved prototypes and opening | Participants investigate from curiosity, revise earlier hypotheses, explain their escape and identify evidence that changed their view of the facility |

If players finish but describe only “I found the teleport switch,” the mystery has not passed. If they understand the truth but cannot execute it without precise camera timing, the spatial design has not passed. If they describe observation contradictions, the law contract has not passed.

Stop after that evaluation. Do not automatically schedule more wings, another mechanic, procedural states or a second mystery. The first complete game should establish that a small, consistent set of laws can sustain the intended revelation.
