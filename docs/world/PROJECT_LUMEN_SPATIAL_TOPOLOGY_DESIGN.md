# Project Lumen — Spatial Topology Design

**Status: design-only topology contract; no scenes, scripts, or gameplay are implemented by this document.** It develops selected Model C from [Elevator State Transition Design](PROJECT_LUMEN_ELEVATOR_STATE_TRANSITION_DESIGN.md), the [Spatial Identity Contract](PROJECT_LUMEN_SPATIAL_IDENTITY_CONTRACT.md), [Facility State Scope Validation](PROJECT_LUMEN_FACILITY_STATE_SCOPE_VALIDATION.md), and [Area Mystery Layout](PROJECT_LUMEN_AREA_MYSTERY_LAYOUT.md). The topology is a **proposed relationship graph**, not proof that its metric geometry, sightlines, one-way lift, room exchange, or unbriefed discoverability work. The existing **REVISE before detailed room design** gate remains in force.

**World rule:** the facility is not one quantum object. A fixed skeleton carries circulation, shaft, socket beds, survey references, and exterior ground. Bounded objects may change placement under the established observation, candidate, rearm, and safety rules. M and N are the **one currently proposed room-scale pair**; their reciprocal P/Q exchange changes which fixed approaches connect. The elevator moves through a continuous fixed shaft into already-existing lower places. It does not switch the whole building, clone rooms, select M@P, or make a watched wall vanish.

## Topology vocabulary and minimal graph

- **Site** means a physically connected research perspective and history, not a parallel map: original operations, the materially duplicated room/work branch, or deep/core infrastructure. Their administrative names may overlap even when their material histories differ.
- **Socket** means a fixed receiving interface, especially P at the upper Atrium and Q at the outer service edge. An address describes the socket, not necessarily the room occupying it.
- **Configuration** means occupancy and connection across the fixed graph. For the proven-design minimum, `N@P, M@Q` and `M@P, N@Q` are the two proposed composite occupations; a room's ordinary door condition is carried with that room.
- **Landing** means a different fixed level on the same down-only shaft. L1–L4 have successively fewer built research approaches. Their difference is **not** a time change of one Atrium.
- **View** and **walkable connection** are separate. A player may see Q, the exterior spur, or a continuous conduit without being able to walk through the intervening fixed structure. No view is silently promoted to a passage.

```text
Awakening --restore--> H0: original upper Atrium
                          |-- Records / Power / Containment / Signal (fixed work routes)
                          |-- P [M or N] -- if M doorway is usable --> X: fixed exterior spur
                          Signal -- fixed survey access/view --> Q [the other shell]

Containment -- lawful A/B/C passenger placement --> C: real exterior yard
C -- ordinary down-only evacuation lift --> L1(4) -> L2(3) -> L3(1) -> L4(0)
                                                               L4 -> safe refuge/egress

Fixed structural/service reference: H0 plant -> P/Q survey spine -> C retaining work
                                    -> lift-shaft footing -> L1 -> L2 -> L3 -> L4 core footing
```

The arrows in the lift row mean **physical downward travel**, not quantum transition. The lower numbers count lateral *research* approaches, excluding the lift threshold and final refuge/egress. The structural/service reference is **not a secret walkable shortcut** from H0 to C: it is an inspectable, continuous fixed datum whose exposed stretches and grilles allow comparison from ordinary accessible viewpoints. Its precise construction and sightlines require a spatial proof before it can support K8.

## Three site identities, one fixed ground

This proposal assigns the sites concrete roles without declaring a second whole building. “Duplicated facility” denotes a **materially distinct research branch and near-matching room structures within one connected facility system**. It does not mean a runtime copy of the original map or identify M as the “real” room and N as the “fake.” The original and duplicated sites are the staff's different work perspectives on fixed contacts and two persistent shells; the core is a deeper foundational perspective.

| Spatial layer / site | Identity and practical purpose | Visible physical features | Accessible areas | Relationship to the others |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Original operations tier** | Awakening, H0, and the four research work approaches form the inhabited upper facility. Staff coordinated surveys, power, handling, and signal work by familiar addresses. | Distinctive fixed atrium plant, vestibule, P contact scar, service conduit, worn work routes, and the known Cube/Beam precedent. | Awakening after its existing restoration; H0; Records, Power, Containment, and Signal via ordinary circulation. C is reachable by the lawful A/B/C supported trip, not by pretending the exterior is an adjacent hallway. | Supplies the fixed baseline against which M/N movement and all later site claims can be measured. Its records may correctly describe a *place* yet misidentify the shell seen there. |
| **Duplicated room/work branch** | A historically near-matched research construction centered on **two materially present room modules M and N** and their service/survey context. Staff repaired them separately; neither is spawned on demand. | M's integrated repair and existing doorway; N's different wound and solid face; bounded floor/wall seams; fixed P and Q beds and Q's outer survey buttress. Ordinary attached conditions travel with their shell, not with an address. | Both shell identities are inspectable from fixed H0/Records/Power and Signal/Q comparison viewpoints in either occupation. A safe passage through M exists where its doorway meets a compatible fixed route; Q's doorway faces a safe blank service recess when M is there. The exact breadth of core work space beyond these fixed viewpoints is **not** specified or needed here. | Gives a third physical perspective on the same fixed reference. It may corroborate K8 if the player can trace the datum and compare it with a known shell/condition; neither descent nor a document alone proves the historical origin. |

The original tier is not “all fixed” while the duplicated tier is “all quantum.” Fixed interfaces exist in both; only explicitly bounded members participate in relocation. A live lamp can differ because M meets another fixed supply, while M's repair and mounted door condition remain M's. A camera mounted to fixed service work observes a participating shell only when its real lens path reaches it; it does not observe or freeze a site label.

## Four elevator-connected configurations

This is a **vertical cross-section of different built places**, not four mutually exclusive world maps. For a concrete, non-tutorial distribution, L1 echoes the four upper research functions; L2 retains Power/Containment/Signal service approaches but has no Records mouth; L3 retains only a Signal/survey service approach; L4 has no lateral research approach. These are **design assignments**, not currently built geography. Every surviving approach must have a believable service purpose; a missing one is an integral wall, not a shutter removed by the lift. The shaft and final egress are never counted as research approaches.

| Landing / site relation | Identity and purpose | Visible features and identity proof | Accessible areas | Relation to upper configuration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 — lower original service, four** | An ordinary logistics distribution hall built to the same institutional central-plan grammar as H0. | Four actual service mouths; a unique patched rib; first lower shaft joint and depth reference. | Fixed short service reaches corresponding to Records, Power, Containment, and Signal functions. These are plausible lower work fronts, **not** four new puzzle wings. Lift continues down. | Initially feels like the upper Atrium revisited. Its different rib and shaft depth prove a second material hall. It preserves the full plan as a comparison baseline. |
| **L2 — duplicated-branch service, three** | A fixed maintenance/survey landing from which staff inspected the near-matching room branch and its contacts. | Records-facing side is continuous wall despite a nearby fixed service contact; different local shear-plate repair; the same shaft and conduit continue past it. | Power, Containment, and Signal service reaches; a fixed survey view/contact trace toward the P/Q branch. No free walk-through from a blank face to M/N is assumed. | Its missing mouth invites “sealed wing” as a first hypothesis. Comparing contact versus face prepares the upper question: did a route disappear, or does this address meet a different structure? |
| **L3 — core approach, one** | Narrow survey/monitoring reach where deep staff could compare outer structures with foundational references. | Only the Signal-like service mouth survives; a cracked footing and exposed shaft joint distinguish L3. Any lens here is visible and its actual target must be inspectable. | One fixed service/survey reach plus down-only lift access to L4. No unseen lateral shortcut to the true route. | The shrinking plan intensifies doubt about assuming four approaches everywhere. It can corroborate a camera or shell history already physically testable above, never supply the first camera rule. |
| **L4 — core refuge, zero** | A protected, physically safe terminus and ordinary egress from the hazardous interior; not a final puzzle chamber. | Central structural motif, unique reverse-bracket repair, continuous shaft footing, no lateral research mouth. | Lift threshold and refuge/egress only. The player survives; this is the Bad Ending's real evacuation outcome. | The same architectural grammar exists without the assumed routes. It leaves a question about the upper P wall but does not prove a quantum change at L4. |

The lift's **down-only** behavior has a legible ordinary cause in its return drive/brake, visible before commitment from C. Stop order is L1 → L2 → L3 → L4. The player may inspect each landing; document fragments are optional and never unlock the next stop. The shaft's continuous rails/depth marks and each landing's independent repair reject an unexplained loop. A lower wall never turns into another doorway while observed. If an unbriefed player concludes the elevator created the walls, the visual proof of fixed landings is insufficient.

The ledger distinguishes **movement of a passenger**, **movement of a bounded room**, and **change of connection**. They can affect the same journey without being one mechanism. No document read, floor choice, or copied map asset substitutes for a physical edge.

## Multiple Atriums: resemblance is not identity

H0 and L1–L4 exist because a large research facility reused a central coordination/utility design at different tiers. H0 was a coordination hall; L1 served lower logistics; L2 surveyed the near-matching room work; L3 supported core monitoring; L4 is a protective refuge. Their common proportions, central footing rhythm, and plant silhouette make a first-time player naturally say “the Atrium.” Their **different material wear and continuous shaft positions** let that hypothesis be corrected without an explanatory map.

| Space | Persistent identity cue | Fixed reference proving where it is | What must not be confused with it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H0** | Unique plant/vestibule wear and P-side pipe scar. | Awakening return route, fixed P approach, upper survey spine. | M or N occupying P; either may form its apparent service face. |
| **L1** | Patched rib and four built service mouths. | First lower shaft joint/depth mark. | H0, despite matching four-direction grammar. |
| **L2** | Shear-plate repair and integral Records-side wall. | Next shaft joint and intact adjacent fixed contact. | A temporary shutter or the same H0 with one door removed. |
| **L3** | Cracked footing and one surviving survey reach. | Deeper shaft joint and service datum continuing downward. | A single room whose three other doors closed during the ride. |
| **L4** | Reverse-bracket repair and terminal footing. | Physical shaft end plus safe refuge/egress. | A quantum “void state” of the upper Atrium. |

The most important identity proof is **not** five decorative icons. The player can follow one fixed shaft and service datum, note successive depth, and find different integrated damage at each stop. No lower landing claims to be the same material space as H0. Conversely, P and Q are **positions**, not room identities; M and N can change which one they occupy. This contrast teaches the player to ask the right question: *same design, same address, or same object?*

## Room duplication: one conserved pair, no runtime copies

**M/N pair.** There are exactly two materially present, bounded, near-matching room modules in the current proposal. Both share a construction grammar and an unusual pre-incident feature; later histories distinguish them. M carries an integrated repaired seam and ordinary mounted doorway/setting. N has a different structural wound, solid face where M has its doorway, and a second attached detail. Either may occupy P or Q, but each keeps its own shell, damage, and ordinary internal state. The exchange is atomic and reciprocal; it occurs only when both current shells and candidate poses are unobserved, safely placeable, and eligible after rearm. A fixed camera seeing a member can hold the pair, but cannot summon either shell.

**“Copied rooms” as historical language.** A near-matched original design and later divergence make duplication a plausible historical claim. Present movement proves **two persistent identities and changing sockets**, not which shell was first, whether the incident created them, or whether an entire alternate facility was copied. Optional Records/core materials may support the origin, but no historical claim is needed to predict the present M@P route. Do not name one “real” and the other “fake” without independent evidence.

**Lower Atrium-like spaces.** L1–L4 repeat an institutional plan but are **not** extra quantum M/N copies, nor four candidate placements for one Atrium. Their progressively fewer approaches are part of their distinct fixed construction. Without this distinction, the Bad Ending would accidentally teach a contradictory whole-facility transformation. Likewise, do not imply every similar office in the duplicated branch is quantum; only authored bounded members with inspectable boundaries can take part in the observation law.

The differences matter causally: M's doorway can align with X only from P, N's solid face cannot; attached door state follows M under K6; P/Q contacts do not. The lower spaces' differing openings matter epistemically: they make a familiar plan unreliable as a material identity, but **alone cannot prove** K7. The player needs the controlled empty M/N exchange against fixed references for that.

## True Ending topology and reachability

The proposed initial occupation is `N@P, M@Q`. A fixed live camera may observe a participating M surface at Q. The player can learn the camera rule earlier at a known A/B/C member, compare M/N identities from fixed H0 and Signal/Q views, and see that X is spatially adjacent to P but not to N's solid face. From fixed ground, the player can therefore predict the consequence of `M@P, N@Q` **before** trying to produce it.

| Composite occupation | Fixed H0/P approach | Q outer service socket | Connection to X |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `N@P, M@Q` | Meets N's damaged solid face. P plant/contact scar does not move. | Holds M's repaired shell and ordinary doorway facing a safe blind recess; its Q contact stays fixed. | No passage from P to X. The lift to lower refuge remains an independent genuine option after reaching C. |
| `M@P, N@Q` | Meets M's repaired shell and its existing door in whatever ordinary condition M carried. | Holds N's distinguishable damaged shell; the same Q buttress/contact remains. | If M's door is usable and geometry safe, ordinary walking through M meets the **fixed** X spur. No portal or final-only unlock. |

To reach the second occupation, the player removes the camera's **actual direct view** of M (not merely a monitor image), accounts for any player/Beam/other camera views of both current shells and their candidate poses, and uses a broad fixed blind return. After a legal unobserved interval, the reciprocal exchange is **permitted, not selected**. The player returns along an observation-preserving fixed path, checks both identities and contacts, and can recover/rearm from either legal resting state. The walk through M to X confirms the predicted relationship. The elevator is not used to choose this occupation; understanding its distinct landings may sharpen the player's model, but a player may reach the True Ending early without descending.

The exterior C route is not fake. X should be **visible but physically separate** from C so the player can form an early question without simply walking around the puzzle. The exact separation, door clearance, camera ray, fixed P↔Q comparison path, broad blind return, and recovery geometry remain spatial proof obligations. If X's separation feels like an arbitrary invisible barrier or if the lower lift becomes the only way to learn a required rule, revise the plan before construction.

## What can be proved, and what remains a proposal

| Claim | Minimum player-visible proof | Current design status |

| **The lift physically descends to four different places.** | Continuous rails/depth, individual integrated repairs, and actual built approach counts. | Topology assigned here; no metric shaft or human test. |
| **The original, duplicated, and core sites share one fixed system.** | A continuous, inspectable survey/service datum from H0 through Q/C/shaft to a core footing, plus one same-shell attached condition compared against fixed contacts from different viewpoints. | Proposed K8 evidence route; continuity and access still require paper spatial proof. Four fragments alone cannot prove it. |
| **M and N are two persistent rooms that exchange.** | Both shells distinguished at P/Q before and after a repeatable empty reciprocal exchange while fixed scar/plant/spur remain. | Spatial identity contract remains **REVISE** pending sightline, safety, and unbriefed tests. |
| **The true route uses a corrected connection.** | M's existing doorway and condition at Q; X fixed beside P; then M@P/N@Q with unchanged fixed frame and safe walk through M to X. | Proposed ending; no final route geometry or player comprehension validated. |

This is the **minimum complete relationship graph** for continued design. It deliberately does not assign meter-scale dimensions, new puzzle mechanics, additional quantum pairs, a whole-facility root, or a complete historical account of duplication. Its central falsifiable promise is that the player can trace fixed references across the sites and then watch their own assumptions about *which material room occupies a familiar address* fail. The intended final understanding is: **“The facility was not changing around me. I moved among different existing arrangements, and one bounded piece of its architecture could change which fixed route it met.”**
