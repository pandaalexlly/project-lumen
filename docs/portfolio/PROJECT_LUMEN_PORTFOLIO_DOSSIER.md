## 1. Project Overview

Project Lumen is a Godot 4.7.2/GDScript first-person, knowledge-driven exploration mystery. Its core premise is that observation constrains where a bounded quantum object can exist. The player makes progress by forming, testing, and revising explanations of that rule, not by collecting upgrades, keys, levels, or statistics. The desired feeling is not merely solving a chamber but discovering that a familiar piece of architecture was misunderstood. The repository currently contains a playable opening, earlier teaching/progression prototypes, isolated mystery probes, technical validators, and extensive design and playtest documentation. It is **not** a finished commercial game or a fully implemented three-facility experience. See [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the [prototype overview](../PROTOTYPE.md), and the [document authority index](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY_INDEX.md).

The planned central cognitive reversal is specific: a player first explains successive familiar atria as **one facility changing under quantum influence**. Later, physical evidence should let them recognize **three fixed facilities that coexisted throughout**; the player had mistaken location and architectural identity. The conclusion must be earned through remembered details, simultaneous observation, depth, and spatial comparison—not a lore reveal, cutscene, or final-answer terminal. Whether a real unbriefed player actually makes that deduction remains **unvalidated**.

The project changed direction during development. An early, mechanically playable Observation Lab → Stabilization rooms → Field Site → Ending sequence tested Cube relocation, artificial observation, destination exclusion, and puzzle flow. Later work shifted the prospective game toward an abandoned-facility exploration mystery. Awakening became the F5 opening; a connected operations atrium and a coherent assembly greybox explored how existing rules might work in a larger world. Subsequent documents explored several spatial-mystery models, including a two-room M/N exchange and a proposed descending-elevator ending. The current [Canonical Spatial Rulebook](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) supersedes the incompatible geography of those proposals. Preserving this history matters: it shows iteration and explicit rejection of attractive but misleading models, not one unchanging master plan.

## 2. Personal Role and Responsibilities

The project record reflects a creator directing a human-led, AI-assisted solo prototype workflow. Task briefs specify design goals, limits, deliverables, and tests; repository artifacts show scenes, scripts, validators, reviews, and documentation created under that direction. The record does **not** by itself prove that the creator personally hand-wrote every line or conducted human playtests. The résumé writer should frame ownership as direction, specification, review, and iteration unless the creator can substantiate a narrower or stronger authorship claim.

**Game direction and scope.** The creator set “knowledge is progression” as the organizing principle, kept the prototype focused on exploration, interaction, and observation, and repeatedly prohibited mechanic creep, tutorial UI, arbitrary locks, and premature final-world production. That demonstrates the ability to define a design pillar, choose what *not* to build, and protect a project's identity through changes in format.

**Puzzle and level design.** Work spans the initial Cube/Beam/Plate progression, environmental discovery in Awakening, fixed-versus-moving spatial references, two-room identity comparison, and candidate final proof viewpoints. The relevant planning skill is constructing a controllable player experiment: a plausible wrong explanation, a physical counterexample, and a later prediction that distinguishes genuine understanding from lucky completion.

**Worldbuilding and narrative structure.** The abandoned research facility is designed to tell its history through repairs, equipment, power distribution, sightlines, and the provenance of records. The proposed Bad Ending treats apparent escape as genuine but incomplete understanding; its documents are optional reinterpretation aids, not collectible gates. This demonstrates environmental narrative design and restraint: lore can contextualize a deduction but cannot stand in for physical proof.

**Player cognition and information design.** Knowledge architectures, evidence contracts, misconception maps, and blind-test rubrics distinguish what a player sees from what they infer. They also distinguish “three visible atria” from “three complete, independently persistent facilities.” This demonstrates attention to player psychology, inference difficulty, mystery pacing, and the danger of a designer mistaking their intended explanation for a player-discovered one.

**Technical prototyping and collaboration.** The project uses Godot scenes and focused GDScript fixtures to put spatial hypotheses under test. The creator supplied detailed requirements and validation boundaries for AI-assisted implementation, then required scene launches, automated checks, preservation of existing systems, and explicit residual risks. This demonstrates technical fluency and cross-disciplinary communication, not a claim that design documentation alone made the prototype playable.

**Validation and documentation management.** The project pairs technical tests with separate human-research plans and maintains a topic-specific document authority index. This demonstrates QA planning, reproducibility, research operations, and the ability to keep changing design decisions intelligible to future collaborators.

## 3. Core Mystery Design

The hardest design problem is not inventing a quantum effect. It is making a *reasonable incorrect model* survive long enough to be meaningful, then allowing the player to refute it independently. Awakening establishes a true but incomplete lesson: an observed Cube holds; loss of relevant observation can permit relocation; a reaching Beam can also observe. This gives the player a productive expectation that anomalous *objects* move inside an otherwise stable building. The later mystery challenges the assumed boundary of “object” and the assumption that a familiar-looking destination is the same physical place.

Current canon distinguishes the bounded exchangeable Left/Right rooms from the three **fixed** facilities. A Left/Right room exchange can demonstrate that a room has identity apart from its socket, but it cannot by itself prove H0, H1, and H2 coexist. Cameras and Beams can support the observer logic, but neither is a magic “facility-state” switch. A final proof must connect prior visits to three persistent facility units visible together. The [rulebook](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) specifies this design truth; the [consistency audit](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_DESIGN_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md) explains where older documents could blur it.

The intended cognition has two gates. First, the lead-in must plausibly produce the player's own one-changing-place account; a moderator may not plant it. Second, simultaneous physical evidence must cause a player holding that account to revise it. Someone who guesses “three facilities” before the contradiction has not experienced the intended reversal, while someone who simply counts three silhouettes afterward has not necessarily mapped three previously visited QER–Atrium–Awakening units. The [cognitive blind run sheet](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_BLIND_PLAYTEST.md) formalizes this distinction. It is a test design, **not evidence that the gates have passed**.

This structure demonstrates puzzle design, information control, and player-model reasoning. It also exposes a real design risk: if the intermediate travels look like ordinary loading or a teleport, or the final panorama reads as one multi-wing building, the authored twist will not be the player's deduction. The project documents that risk rather than declaring the mystery solved.

## 4. World and Spatial Design

The current canonical map has three separate, fixed vertical facility units. H0, H1, and H2 are their respective atria; each is connected within its own unit to a fixed Quantum Entanglement Room (QER) above and an Awakening space below:

```text
Facility 1             Facility 0             Facility 2
   QER1                   QER0                   QER2
     |                      |                      |
     H1                     H0                     H2
     |                      |                      |
 Awakening1             Awakening0             Awakening2

State A, west to east:
Left Room′ — H1 — Left Room — H0 — Right Room — H2 — Right Room′

State B: only the two inner Left/Right room identities exchange positions.
```

Left Room′ and Right Room′ remain fixed outer comparison rooms. Only Left Room and Right Room reciprocally exchange the two inner positions, keeping their participating structure and current attached conditions. H0/H1/H2, their QERs, and their Awakening spaces do not transform or trade places. A fixed address, a room shell's material identity, and a facility unit's identity are therefore three different questions. A changed frontage at one fixed interface may indicate a different room occupant; it does **not** establish that the atrium itself moved, or that there is only one facility.

QERs are fixed entry points into quantum rooms under the design canon; they are not automatically moving rooms. QER-mediated entry, Left/Right room exchange, and supported-passenger travel are separate concepts. Several production contracts remain open: QER destination selection, exact door faces and one-way egress, loose-object membership, authored exchange sightlines, and the final player-facing coexistence proof. The current triple-coexistence probes deliberately omit the four horizontal rooms. Their floor-level player handoffs simulate interrupted travel for a cognition test; they are **not** the QER implementation. These boundaries are important evidence of systemic level-design discipline: an isolated experiment does not quietly become world canon.

Earlier M/N rooms at A/B or P/Q positions were valuable tests of room identity versus location. Their geography is **not** a one-to-one alias of current Left/Right and H0/H1/H2. Likewise, older “original/duplicated/core” topology documents and the proposed elevator-connected configurations are design history, not proof that a whole building changes quantum state. The [authority index](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY_INDEX.md) records how to use each without erasing the work.

## 5. Player Cognitive Route Design

The intended experience is **initial misunderstanding → accumulated evidence → contradiction → reinterpretation → physical proof**. The sequence is about a changing mental model, not mandatory “one room teaches one mechanic” lessons. Awakening supplies a trustworthy baseline. Repaired components and attached settings can then suggest that an object carries identity and current condition across relocation. A powered camera's live sightline may reframe mundane surveillance as observation, but the camera must be tested as a sightline, not a switch. Distinct room repairs beside fixed plant or receiver scars invite the next distinction: *which part is the thing that moved?* Finally, remembered H0/H1/H2 structural histories must be matched to simultaneously visible fixed stacks. The [cognitive integration plan](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CORE_MYSTERY_COGNITIVE_INTEGRATION.md) describes the earlier small-scale transfer tests; the [canonical rulebook](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) governs their eventual three-facility interpretation.

The evidence hierarchy is deliberate. A player should first see or do something, then consider a natural wrong explanation, then encounter a controlled contradiction, then make a prospective prediction. In the persistence fixture, a module returns to its old receiver with its *new* lever condition, defeating a snapshot-recall theory. In the camera fixture, the same powered lens stops counting when its optical path is physically blocked, defeating a simple power-switch account. In M/N, two repaired shells trade fixed positions while exterior references do not move, defeating “only the door changed.” In the final proof, prior atrium identities must be present together, defeating “one atrium changed state.” This is stronger than a document announcing the law.

Documents are designed to be distributed among work sites and personnel, including ordinary records, honestly mistaken interpretations, and later contradictions. Records is not an answer room. The proposed Bad Ending's four fragments would make previously seen evidence newly interesting on replay; reading them would not unlock the truth or substitute for physical observation. The **function** of these fragments remains relevant to current design authority, but the older four-stop elevator route and its inherited M/N/P/Q geography have not been reconciled with the current three-stack map or implemented. Replay reinterpretation is an intended emotional outcome, **not a measured player response**. See the [knowledge architecture V2](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE_V2.md) and [Bad Ending proposal](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_BAD_ENDING_AND_REPLAY_STRUCTURE.md), read through the authority index.

This work demonstrates narrative design, unobtrusive onboarding, hypothesis-driven environmental storytelling, and information pacing. Its strongest production principle is that optional writing may enrich *why* a place exists, while the required causal insight remains available through the world.

## 6. Prototype Development

The project is organized around `scenes/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, and `docs/`. The main scene in [`project.godot`](../../project.godot) is `res://scenes/world/opening/awakening_chamber.tscn`; the mystery probes run directly from `scenes/tests/` and do not replace F5. Reusable observation and relocation code remains in `scripts/observation/`; isolated probe scripts live under `scripts/world/`, with validators under `tests/world/` or `scripts/world/`. The source is Godot 4.7.2/GDScript, using the existing first-person controller and E interaction where a fixture requires it. The repository holds both tracked changes and untracked prototype material; a Git commit alone is not a reproducible record of every current probe. The [pilot snapshot manifest](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PILOT_SNAPSHOT_MANIFEST.md) and [freeze sign-off](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PILOT_FREEZE_SIGNOFF.md) address that release risk for the cognitive probe.

**Earlier playable work.** The Observation Lab and Stabilization rooms mechanically exercised observation, Beam stabilization, destination exclusion, failure/recovery, and a Field Site transfer of the rules; they remain prototype content, not the final exploration world. Awakening then became a more believable abandoned research chamber with a Cube experiment, Beam/load-plate interplay, facility restoration, and an environmental discovery flow. F5 enters Awakening, whose restoration opens into an Operations Atrium with a central plant and four physically sealed research approaches. A connected coherent-assembly greybox later placed one bounded Cube/bearer/cradle/collar object at three receivers with real Beam controls, passenger-support checks, and fixed access. Its technical tests passed, but neither its final-world meaning nor its human discoverability was proven. See the [prototype overview](../PROTOTYPE.md), [Awakening discovery flow](../world/AWAKENING_DISCOVERY_FLOW.md), [hub foundation](../world/CENTRAL_HUB_FOUNDATION.md), and [connected assembly validation](../world/TASK038B_ASSEMBLY_WORLD_INTEGRATION.md).

**M/N spatial identity probe.** The question was whether a player could track two whole room shells independently of two fixed locations. A standalone greybox gives each shell a long-distance structural repair, a medium-distance confirmation, and a close detail. Fixed plant, receiver rims, a rib, and a stack provide comparison. When both rooms are unobserved and the player is safely outside, the pair reciprocally exchanges offscreen; watching either participating shell holds the pair. Automated checks passed for two-shell movement, fixed references, observation hold, rearm, and safe occupancy. This illustrates spatial-evidence design: make the moving identity and fixed address separately observable. **Not established:** that unbriefed players notice both identities, predict the reciprocal result, or reject a door-animation/teleport reading. [Validation record](../world/MN_SPATIAL_IDENTITY_PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md).

**Quantum State Persistence probe.** The question was whether moving an object preserves what *currently happened to it*, not a receiver-owned or older snapshot state. A repaired module with an attached lever moves between fixed A/B beds. At B, the player can switch the lever OFF; after a return to A, the lever remains OFF and A's fixed lamp stays dark. A normal lever action can relight it. Automated checks passed for same-instance identity, carried repair and lever setting, fixed contact, rearm, E interaction, and the B-OFF → A-OFF counterexample. This illustrates causal puzzle design: one ordinary, reversible action differentiates present-state continuity from time rewind. **Not established:** that a human player notices the attached-versus-fixed distinction or predicts the counterexample before seeing it. [Validation record](../world/STATE_PERSISTENCE_PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md).

**Camera Observation probe.** The question was whether a live CCTV lens could count as an observer without becoming a hidden switch. The isolated fixture reuses the persistence module and adds a powered camera aimed at the A-side member. An opaque maintenance shroud interrupts the *actual optical ray* while power and module condition remain unchanged. Technical checks passed: camera-only sight holds, blocked camera sight no longer counts, player sight still holds when the camera is blocked, and object identity/setting survive a lawful move and recovery. This illustrates experimental control and transfer of an existing rule to ordinary environmental equipment. The scene contains no Beam and does not validate a final camera-based ending. **Not established:** whether players interpret the camera as an observer rather than a cover-operated lock. [Validation record](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CAMERA_OBSERVATION_VALIDATION.md).

**Triple Coexistence proof and cognitive probes.** The base greybox has three fixed QER–Atrium–Awakening placeholders and two walkable H0 viewpoints: A, an internal elevated gallery, and B, an exterior ground-level apron. B technically frames all three stacks most clearly; A may read as a bridge. Validators passed fixed-transform, sightline, parallax, floor, and controller traversal checks. A later cognitive wrapper adds H0 → H1 → H2 → H0 prior visits through occluded floor-level handoffs, then returns the player to the A/B proof approaches. Technical validation passed for route, fixed simultaneous stacks, sampled pre-proof occlusion, and viewpoint access. The paired probes illustrate scope control: test the final sightline separately, then test whether earlier experience gives it cognitive meaning. Their deterministic handoffs are research scaffolding, **not** canonical QER travel. Neither prototype has established that a player forms the intended wrong model or revises it on seeing the stacks. [Greybox result](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_GREYBOX_VALIDATION.md); [cognitive result](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md).

These prototypes demonstrate an ability to reduce a large narrative ambition to falsifiable, cheap experiments. The valuable output is not merely the functioning scene; it is the explicit boundary between what that scene measured and what it could not.

## 7. Validation-Driven Development Process

The recurring method is **design hypothesis → isolated prototype → automated technical validation → blind-test protocol → analysis framework**. Hypotheses are written as claims a player could test: for example, “a fixed receiver does not remember an object's old lever state,” or “a simultaneously visible structure corresponds to a previously visited location.” Geometry and scripts then isolate the relevant variable. Headless import, direct scene launch, validators, regression checks, rendered inspection where available, and whitespace checks establish that the *test apparatus* works. They do not establish salience, comprehension, comfort, emotional payoff, or a population-level result.

For room identity, the M/N audit asks whether a player inspected *both* shells before exchange and can predict both after exchange while naming a fixed reference. For observation, the camera test holds power constant and changes only line of sight, guarding against a misleading switch explanation. For the final mystery, the cognitive probe measures the **pre-proof belief** separately from the **post-proof account**. It records volunteered statements during visits (`T0`), an H2 map before return (`T1`), any insight after returning to H0 but before the panorama (`T2`), the locked first-view interpretation (`T3`), and later diagnostic discussion (`T4`). A/B assigned route is distinguished from actual first revealing viewpoint. The [blind run sheet](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_BLIND_PLAYTEST.md) and [mental-model scoring revision](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_MENTAL_MODEL_SCORING_REVISION.md) require more than correct vocabulary: a strong account maps the three remembered identities and their upper/lower relationships to fixed, simultaneous units.

The research plan also protects against false positives. It distinguishes a spontaneous theory from one elicited by sketching, a proof-view deduction from a T2 early insight, and a player inference from moderator influence. It anticipates “three ordinary wings,” “another quantum state,” “teleportation,” and “I do not know” as diagnostically different outcomes. Two **unscored logistics pilots** are planned to check that the procedure can capture comparable evidence before formal blind sessions; pilots would not validate the puzzle. The current [freeze sign-off](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PILOT_FREEZE_SIGNOFF.md) records a verified reproducible ZIP and headless runs but remains **NO-GO pending on-site setup and human logistics**. No unbriefed result is recorded. This is a strong example of data-driven design discipline precisely because the project does not convert a technical pass into a human claim.

## 8. Documentation and Design Management

The [Canonical Spatial Rulebook](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) is the current authority for what is fixed, what exchanges, and what remains unresolved. The [Document Authority Index](../world/PROJECT_LUMEN_DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY_INDEX.md) distinguishes canon, supporting intent, isolated technical records, operative playtest documents, and deprecated geography. This matters because several older, compelling proposals use the same vocabulary while describing incompatible maps. Without explicit precedence, a collaborator could quietly combine one-H0 M/N/P/Q topology, a quantum elevator, floor-level test handoffs, and the new three-stack canon into an impossible implementation.

Validation documents state exact fixture assumptions and results rather than only the design aspiration. A [consistency audit](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_DESIGN_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md) identified stale links, ambiguous proof thresholds, and the difference between recognizing three atrium cues and recognizing three complete facilities. Playtest material comprises a current moderator run sheet, pilot checklist, session tracking sheet, result-analysis template, mental-model scoring revision, pilot-review guide, execution checklist, freeze checklist/sign-off, artifact manifest, and run log. The record separates raw observation from interpretation and preserves the authority of current materials over an older proof-only protocol. See the [session sheet](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PLAYER_SESSION_TRACKING_SHEET.md) and [pilot review guide](../world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PILOT_RESULT_REVIEW_GUIDE.md).

This is production-oriented documentation practice: make decisions traceable, label a proposal as a proposal, record when a model is superseded, preserve test reproducibility, and give a new contributor a reliable way to tell “implemented,” “verified,” and “intended” apart. It is **not** evidence that a multi-person production team has shipped the game using these documents.

## 9. Technical Collaboration with AI Tools

Codex was used as implementation and documentation assistance in a tightly specified workflow. The creator's briefs defined target player cognition, canonical boundaries, prohibited expansions, deliverables, and test obligations. AI assistance helped turn those specifications into Godot greyboxes, GDScript fixtures/validators, audits, and structured documentation. The creator retained design direction by deciding what hypotheses deserved a prototype, revising the mystery from earlier models to the current canon, and requiring explicit status and limits in each handoff. The repository's scripts and design files should be reviewed as **AI-assisted project artifacts**, not represented as code or prose unquestionably written unaided by one person.

This collaboration illustrates useful modern development skills: translating an ambiguous creative goal into a testable technical brief, communicating to a coding partner across design/engineering boundaries, protecting existing systems during experiments, checking implementation claims against source and validators, and managing documentation drift. It does **not** show that AI proved the mystery works. The most consequential open question—whether people infer three fixed facilities without being told—requires unbriefed human observation.

## 12. Interview Discussion Points

**Why make the first belief wrong?** Discuss how Awakening teaches a true observation rule while encouraging an incomplete boundary assumption. A fair twist should reinterpret, not invalidate, what the player learned.

**How can a puzzle be fair without explaining its rule?** Walk through one controlled contradiction: B-OFF → A-OFF for persistence, powered camera with clear versus blocked line of sight, or two M/N identities changing fixed positions. Explain why a before-action prediction is stronger evidence of understanding than retrospective completion.

**Why insist on physical proof of three facilities?** Contrast simultaneous fixed stacks, remembered repairs, and parallax with a document saying “there are three.” Explain why a Left/Right exchange or three visible atria alone is insufficient.

**What did the prototypes actually teach the team?** They established technical feasibility and exposed interpretation risks: A's bridge-like framing, B's ordinary-wing reading, receiver handoffs that may feel like loading, a camera cover that may feel like a switch, and repair cues that may not carry across viewpoints. They have **not** yet supplied human comprehension data.

**How do you know when to revise?** Discuss the distinction between a broken scene, a confusing route, a weak identity cue, an ambiguous viewpoint, and an overly difficult deduction. Explain why the two logistics pilots test the *measurement procedure* before formal sessions test the mystery.

**How did the design change without losing source of truth?** Describe the shift from tutorial-like vertical slice to exploration, the earlier M/N/elevator proposals, the later three-fixed-facility rulebook, and the document authority index that prevents incompatible geography from leaking into new work.

**What was your role with AI assistance?** Be concrete about the creator's specifications, review decisions, technical checks, and limits. Name what Codex assisted with and what still requires direct human judgment. The strongest account is an honest one: a testable design process and working prototypes, with the central player revelation still awaiting blind validation.
