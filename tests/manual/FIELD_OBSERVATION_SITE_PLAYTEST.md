# Field Observation Site Playtest

Reach the Field Observation Site through the F5 progression for the primary
test. It can also be run directly for focused iteration.

## A. Developer smoke test

- Inspect the initial view. Confirm the damaged entrance wing, ceiling ribs,
  familiar QuantumDoor, warm entry light, and glimpse into the operations hall
  establish an abandoned facility without tutorial text.
- Observe the QuantumDoor, look away for the normal release interval, and
  confirm it parks beside the entry yard. Confirm the opening is traversable
  and the parked destination rejects relocation while occupied by the Player.
- Enter the ruined operations hall. Confirm its abandoned console bank,
  observation-window framing, east control annex, powered conduit, containment
  shield, emitter gantry, west mezzanine, restoration relay, and closed service
  exit read as parts of one former research facility.
- Deliberately release the Cube from a non-solution ground view and confirm a
  wrong result can be re-observed and recovered without `R`.
- Confirm the Field Cube retains ordinary systemic destination selection; it
  does not inherit the Combined chamber's orange-first teaching tie-break.
- Use the east control annex to redirect the enabled Beam from the containment
  cradle to the framed receiver dock.
- Find the broken west-side breach, climb the old access ramp, and use the
  observation mezzanine. From roughly `(-8.5, 2.9, -4.5)`, keep the archive
  niche visible while the containment shield hides the cradle and restoration
  relay.
- From nearby mezzanine positions, confirm Start is hidden and outside the
  Beam, the archive niche is directly visible, the receiver dock is
  Beam-covered, and the restoration relay is hidden. The safe set should be
  Goal only.
- Confirm the Cube reaches the restoration relay, the machinery Plate
  activates, and the northern service gate opens. Follow the return ramp into
  the service exit.
- Confirm the service exit reaches the dedicated ending and `R` there revisits
  the Field Site.

## B. Knowledge-transfer playtest

Prerequisite: the tester has completed the current full knowledge arc.

Tell them only:

> You've reached another area.
> Find a way through.

Record:

- whether they recognize QuantumDoor behavior without prompting;
- whether they identify the Beam as the same observation system;
- whether the broken west route and observation mezzanine are discovered;
- whether they use player gaze as an active candidate constraint;
- whether they think in terms of eliminating futures;
- whether they brute-force repeated relocations;
- whether every wrong state can be re-observed and released without using `R`;
- whether the space feels like a place or another test chamber;
- whether restoring the relay and opening the service exit read as one
  environmental reaction.

After completion ask:

> What did you do to control where the anomaly could appear?

Record the explanation without suggesting an answer.

## C. Level-feel questions

Record:

- Did the area feel too empty or too obviously constructed as a puzzle?
- Was there too much walking or too little exploration?
- Was the useful observation mezzanine discoverable?
- Was the Beam aim readable at distance?
- Did the machinery and service-gate reaction clearly communicate success?
- Were wrong Cube states recoverable without frustration?
