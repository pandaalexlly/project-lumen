# Triple Coexistence — Blind Playtest Result Analysis Template

**Blank analysis framework; no human sessions are reported here. Human comprehension is UNVALIDATED.** The [cognitive blind protocol](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_BLIND_PLAYTEST.md) defines the prompts, timing, eligibility, and primary scoring rules; the [pilot checklist](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_PILOT_CHECKLIST.md) must establish that the procedure is usable before scored sessions. This template interprets collected evidence; it does not change the prototype, puzzle, or protocol after seeing results.

## 1. Evidence and player mental models

Analyze **two independent gates**. `G1`: did the H0 → H1 → H2 visits produce a participant-generated belief that one place changed? `G2`: among participants who still held that belief immediately before the proof, did the first adequate triple view make them revise it using physical evidence? A correct answer without `G1` is not the intended reversal.

Use the protocol's timeline: `T0` = volunteered account during visits; `T1` = H2 pre-return sketch and last pre-proof account; `T2` = H0-return account before exterior view; `T3` = first-view account locked before crossover; `T4` = second view/debrief, diagnostic only. Preserve each participant's exact words and sketch alongside analyst codes. Do not infer an unspoken T0 belief from silence.

| Mental model | What the player believes, regardless of vocabulary | Distinguishing evidence in sketch, explanation, or prediction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One changing facility** (`B1` at baseline) | Successive atria are the **same physical place** in different conditions or configurations. “The repairs changed each time I passed through” qualifies. | One atrium drawn repeatedly or one site with sequential versions; predicts that returning finds another condition of that same site. Merely noticing similar architecture is insufficient. |
| **Multiple fixed facilities** (`B2` at baseline) | Distinct persistent places exist at once; travel changes the player's location, not each place's identity. | Separate persistent sites in the map, tied to earlier visits and, after proof, observable together. They may still be unsure *how* travel works. |
| **Ordinary multi-wing building** (`B2` or `B4`, depending on the map) | The player may mean either three separate persistent QER–atrium–Awakening units in a larger complex, **or** three adjacent rooms/wings without recognizing distinct visited units. | Code the **physical model first**: if each prior visit maps to a separate enduring stack, treat “wing” as harmless wording (`B2`); if it is one ordinary building with no mapped stack identities, record another model (`B4`) or a partial post-view account. |
| **Quantum-state explanation** (`B1` or `B4`, depending on what changes) | The player may say one atrium cycles through states, or that several fixed places have quantum behavior. The word “quantum” does not settle the map. | Ask of the recorded account: *what persists, what moves, and what exists simultaneously?* One physical atrium replacing itself is `B1`; a different, clearly specified topology is `B4` until its mapping is established. At T3, a one-site cycling account has **not** accepted the contradiction. |
| **Uncertain / no model** (`B3`) | The player names observations but makes no stable claim about place identity. | Multiple incompatible sketches, “I don't know,” or no prediction. Do not force uncertainty into a wrong-model or correct-model count. |

Record competing explanations without “correcting” the participant's nouns. A model can change within a phase: note the first statement, the last statement, and the observation or prompt between them. Two analysts should code recordings and sketches independently before reconciling disagreements with timestamps and quotes; when practical, conceal assigned A/B route from the first pass.

## 2. Interpret success without rewarding a guess

Use the protocol's component codes at `T3`: identity `I` (none / some / all three prior visits mapped), coexistence `C` (no / uncertain / yes), evidence `E` (none / one type / both remembered identity and physical coexistence/topology), and revision `R` (no / tentative / evidence-backed rejection of the player's former map). Report whether a conclusion was **spontaneous** (`S`), produced only after a permitted neutral question/sketch (`N`), made before the view (`T2`), or **moderator-influenced**.

| Outcome | Required interpretation | Do not count as this outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full proof-view reversal** | Clean `B1` at the last pre-proof account (`T1`), no correction at `T2`; adequate first view; at locked `T3`, `I=all three`, `C=yes`, `E=both`, `R=evidence-backed`; no answer-bearing help. The player connects the **previously visited** identities to three simultaneous, materially separated stacks and explains why the prior one-place model fails. Report `S` and `N` separately. | Saying “three facilities” without mapped visits or reasons; a conclusion first reached on H0 return; a T4/debrief answer. |
| **Partial understanding** | The player recognizes multiple locations but cannot map all prior visits, sees three stacks but does not establish identity, or tentatively revises the old model using only one evidence type. An unsupported correct-count guess also remains partial. | Treating partial as a full reversal merely because the terminology matches the design document. |
| **Failure after adequate exposure** | A clean, adequately exposed player cannot identify the contradiction, continues to explain the view as one site cycling through states, or gives another unsupported explanation without a mapped identity/coexistence account. | No adequate view, early sightline leak, recording failure, or moderator-supplied answer: classify these as indeterminate/contaminated, not cognitive failures. |
| **Early correct / no reversal opportunity** | `B2` before proof or evidence-backed correction at `T1`/`T2`. Valuable `G1` or return-beat evidence, but the first A/B view did not cause a wrong-to-right transition. | Excluding these players from the recruitment and baseline report because they were “too clever.” |

Success is a **causal account**, not a password. “Three wings” may be fully correct if the participant maps each visit onto a persistent, separate stack. “Three facilities” may be a guess if they cannot connect the view to earlier visits. Confidence is supporting context, not a pass criterion: compare the player's own `T1` and `T3` wording only if both were elicited with the same neutral prompt; report higher / same / lower / unclear, without inferring certainty from volume or speed.

## 4. Group summary and denominators

Fill this before writing a narrative conclusion. Show raw counts, not just percentages; the first wave is exploratory rather than a population estimate.

| Measure | Count / notes |
| --- | --- |
| Recruited; completed; eligible; excluded/contaminated **by reason**; clean baseline visits |  |
| `T0` volunteered one-changing / multiple-fixed / uncertain / other |  |
| `T1` last pre-proof `B1/B2/B3/B4`; how many `B1` first appeared only with the sketch |  |
| `T2` corrections before first exterior view |  |
| Clean `B1` at T1 **and** no T2 correction **and** adequate first view: `G2` denominator |  |
| `T3` full `S`; full `N`; partial; failure; indeterminate/contaminated, each with reason |  |
| Actual first A/B/en-route exposure, duration, and early crossover; assigned-versus-actual mismatch |  |
| Repeated identity, coexistence/depth, lead-in, navigation, or recording failures |  |

`G1` is the distribution of baseline models among **all eligible participants with clean prior visits**; report T0-volunteered and T1-sketch-elicited `B1` separately. `G2` uses only clean `B1` cases whose model persisted through T2 and who received an adequate first view. Also report the full recruited flow so early-correct, uncertain, and contaminated sessions remain visible. Split A/B by **assigned route and actual first adequate view**; treat comparisons as inconclusive if leakage, crossover, or exposure duration differs materially. Do not infer a viewpoint effect from one memorable participant.

## 5. Diagnose failures from observable evidence

Treat each row as a **hypothesis to test**, not an automatic design verdict. Check exposure, prior account, prompts, and rival explanations before choosing a revision.

| Observed pattern | Evidence supporting a diagnosis | Rival explanation / discriminating check | Appropriate *future* revision if repeated clean cases support it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Three facilities guessed before proof** | Multiple-site sketch or prediction at T0/T1, before answer-bearing input. | Spoiler? Early triple sightline? Receiver handoff reads as transport? Or a genuine inference from legitimate differences? Check video and prior exposure; an early correct participant is not a failed player. | If legitimate early inference is common, revisit the **pre-reveal sequence** and cue timing. If leakage or moderator input caused it, fix the test procedure first. Do not weaken clues based on one case. |
| **Sees several spaces, calls them one large building** | T3 sketch has multiple adjacent spaces but no one-to-one link between H0/H1/H2 visits and fixed QER–atrium–Awakening stacks. | If the player *does* map each visit to a separate persistent unit, “building/wing” is only vocabulary. Check the sketch and retrace prediction before coding a failure. | If mapping repeatedly fails despite cue exposure, revise **identity continuity** or the visibility of each complete stack relationship; not the player's noun. |
| **Keeps “another quantum state” after proof** | Clean `B1` persists at T3 despite an adequate view; explanation still treats one physical site as replacing itself. | Did the player actually attend to all three, move for parallax, and see depth/separation? Did transfer scaffolding encourage a load/state interpretation? | If mapped identities are sound but coexistence is missed in one approach, revise that **proof viewpoint**. If exposure was poor or inconsistent, fix measurement/navigation first. |
| **Does not understand the reveal** | Uncertain map or conflicting account at T3; misses or cannot retain repair cues; no clear spatial prediction. | Separate unclear identity cues from confusing viewpoint, short exposure, controls/navigation, or excessive cognitive load. Compare first-noticed detail, cue frames, sketches, and intervention log across cases. | Revise the **specific failed link**: identity clues when prior places cannot be recognized; viewpoint when recognized places do not read as simultaneous; pre-reveal sequence when no stable baseline forms. |
| **“Correct” answer without evidence** | Player counts three or repeats a phrase but cannot map visits or cite independent identity plus topology evidence. | Could a neutral prompt or T4 exploration later reveal a real model? Keep later understanding diagnostic; it cannot rewrite T3. | No design change from this alone. Record as partial/guess and look for a repeated evidence gap. |

## 6. Revision decision record

Apply the [blind protocol's first-wave decision gate](../../tests/manual/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_BLIND_PLAYTEST.md#7-decision-gate-after-the-first-wave), not a new threshold invented after seeing data. Its six-clean-`B1` minimum and roughly two-thirds provisional-retention guide are **triage rules**, not proof of general player comprehension. Document exceptions and rival explanations before any future design task.

| Decision | Required pattern before recommending it | Evidence to cite; what **not** to conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep current geometry for another blind round** | Sufficient clean `B1`/adequate-view cases; repeated evidence-backed T3 reversals from both actual approaches with comparable exposure; no dominant recurrent identity, depth, or navigation problem. | Cite denominators and disconfirming cases. Say **provisionally retain**, not “validated for all players.” |
| **Modify identity clues** | Repeated clean players see several structures but fail to associate H1/H2 exteriors with their own earlier visits **despite recorded cue opportunity**. | Cite prior and final cue frames, sketch mappings, and quotes. Do not change the final viewpoint solely because players used “wing.” |
| **Modify proof viewpoint** | Players retain prior identities but adequate first view fails to establish simultaneous material separation, especially a replicated A-versus-B bridge/screen/state reading with comparable exposure. | Cite actual, not merely assigned, first views; parallax behavior; exposure duration; surviving rival model. Do not claim an A/B difference if early crossover or route leakage dominates. |
| **Modify pre-reveal sequence** | The intended `B1` rarely forms, forms only after sketch prompting, or correct multiplicity is frequently inferred before proof from legitimate visits. | Cite T0/T1 distributions, early views, and handoff interpretations. Do not judge the final view using players who had no wrong model to revise. |
| **Defer any design change** | Too few clean `B1`/adequate-view cases, conflicting diagnoses, moderator contamination, unusable recording, or a one-off unclear response. | Fix the procedure or collect another blind wave. Preserve raw cases and uncertainty. |
