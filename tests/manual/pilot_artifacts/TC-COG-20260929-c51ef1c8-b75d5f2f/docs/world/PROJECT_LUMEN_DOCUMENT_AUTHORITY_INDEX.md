# Project Lumen — Document Authority Index

**Status:** navigation and precedence index for the current three-facility mystery; not a new world-rule contract. It records the distinctions found in the [Triple Coexistence consistency audit](TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_DESIGN_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md). The isolated prototypes are technically validated; **unbriefed-player comprehension remains UNVALIDATED**. Do not treat a later file date, an implementation shortcut, or an attractive older proposal as an automatic canon change.

## How to use this index

For work on the three-facility mystery, read this index and the [Canonical Spatial Rulebook](PROJECT_LUMEN_CANONICAL_SPATIAL_RULEBOOK.md) first, then the relevant supporting or prototype records. An explicit, later authorized canon decision would supersede the rulebook; **no such decision is recorded here**. Until then:

1. The rulebook controls **what exists and what can move**. Its `UNRESOLVED` items remain unresolved; another document's plausible geometry is not permission to fill them in.
2. A current playtest protocol controls **how that test is run and scored**, not the world's physics. A scene and its validation notes control **what the isolated prototype actually does**, not what future QER transfer must do.
3. Supporting documents may explain *why* a design is interesting or how to look for evidence. They cannot override the map, establish an untested player inference, or turn an optional document into a gate.
4. Archived material remains searchable design history. If it conflicts with current canon, identify the conflict and ask for a recorded decision rather than silently mapping old names to new ones.

Authority is **topic-specific**, not a single ranking for every question. In particular, technical validator success is not human-readability validation, and a pilot checklist is not a spatial rulebook.

## Rules currently frozen by the spatial canon

- **Three distinct fixed facilities coexist.** Facility 0 contains QER0–H0–Awakening0; Facility 1 contains QER1–H1–Awakening1; Facility 2 contains QER2–H2–Awakening2. Their atria, QERs, and Awakening spaces do not replace one another or switch whole-facility states.
- **Horizontal State A:** Left Room′ — H1 — Left Room — H0 — Right Room — H2 — Right Room′. Only **Left Room and Right Room** may reciprocally exchange the two inner positions. Left Room′ and Right Room′ remain fixed at the outer positions. State B is the inner pair reversed; no atrium or whole facility moves.
- **QERs are fixed entry points.** QER-mediated arrival inside a quantum room is distinct from Left/Right room exchange and from supported-passenger movement. The exact QER destination and door/egress contracts remain unresolved in the rulebook; do not import an old P/Q route or prototype handoff as their answer.
- **The final three-facility conclusion needs direct physical evidence.** Records, camera/Beam behavior, and Left/Right exchange may prepare or corroborate understanding, but neither a document nor a changed doorway alone proves that H0/H1/H2 coexist. A player must be able to compare persistent place identities with simultaneous fixed structures.
- **Bad Ending fragments are reinterpretation aids, not progression gates.** Reading, collecting, or combining them is not a requirement for the physical truth or a true route.

These are design rules, **not** a claim that final facility geometry, all four quantum rooms, production QER access, endings, or the intended revelation have been implemented or human-validated.

## Handoff rule for future work

Before creating a new room, route, validator, or playtest instruction, state which canonical rule it relies on and which prototype shortcut—if any—it replaces. If an older file and the rulebook disagree, cite both and stop at the unresolved boundary; do not repair the conflict by renaming entities, adding an invisible gate, or moving a fixed facility. If a test note and the actual isolated scene disagree, inspect the scene and report documentation drift without promoting the scene behavior to canon.

In particular, the rulebook still leaves **QER destination/eligibility, room door faces and one-way egress, loose internal-object membership, authored exchange geometry, opening occupation, extent of each facility beyond its stack, and production-ready coexistence proof** unresolved. The technically working A/B proof views are candidates; two logistics pilots and subsequent unbriefed sessions have not established the intended cognitive reversal. Keep **technical validation** and **human comprehension** in separate status lines until real results exist.
