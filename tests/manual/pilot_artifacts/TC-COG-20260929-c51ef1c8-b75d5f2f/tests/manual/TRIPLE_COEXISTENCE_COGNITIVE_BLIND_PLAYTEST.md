# Triple Coexistence Cognitive Probe — First Blind Playtest Run Sheet

**Status: ready for logistics pilots; no human sessions recorded. Human comprehension is UNVALIDATED.** Use the isolated `res://scenes/tests/triple_coexistence_cognitive_probe.tscn`, not F5. This run sheet executes the [cognitive scenario](../../docs/world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_VALIDATION_SCENARIO.md) with the current [prototype](../../docs/world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md). It supersedes the older [proof-only blind protocol](../../docs/world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_BLIND_PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md) wherever that protocol says prior H1/H2 visits are unavailable or suggests a question that presupposes a changed map. Keep its distinction between A/B *assigned route* and *actual first view*.

## 1. Study question, sample, and eligibility

Measure two separate gates: **G1**, did H0 → H1 → H2 naturally produce the participant's own “one place changing” account; **G2**, did the final simultaneous view make a participant who held that account replace it with three fixed, previously visited units? A technical pass answers neither gate. An early correct theory is valuable evidence about G1, not a reason to remove the participant.

- Run **two unscored logistics pilots**, then a first exploratory wave of **12–16 unbriefed adults**, with 6–8 assigned A-first and 6–8 B-first by a concealed, balanced random list. These are design-triage numbers, not a statistical validation sample. Recruit independently; participants must not watch or discuss other sessions.
- All primary participants must have the **same playable, nonverbal prior observation experience** (use the existing Awakening opening only, or a previously completed equivalent fixed in advance for the entire wave). Do not tell them observation/quantum rules. Do not mix different primers across A/B. Record whether they actually noticed those effects; lack of understanding is an outcome, not automatic exclusion.
- Exclude from the *blind-inference* analysis only prior exposure to this probe, its screenshots, developer documents, the three-facility answer, or another session; answer-bearing moderator help; or a technical fault that prevents prior visits or an adequate reveal. Keep all such sessions in a recruitment/deviation log. FPS familiarity, slow travel, uncertainty, an early correct inference, and wrong answers are **not** exclusions. Offer normal accessibility accommodations and record any changed FOV, brightness, controls, or motion comfort.
- Screen for spoilers before the game is visible: **“Have you seen or discussed any material from this Project Lumen test before today? What did you see?”** Do not list possible answers. Obtain recording consent and assign an anonymous ID. Do not record identifiable information in the observation sheet.

## 2. Room and build setup — private moderator checklist

1. Use the same build, monitor, resolution, camera FOV, brightness, control mapping, and prior playable experience throughout the wave. Launch the cognitive scene **directly as a game window**, not in the editor. Hide filenames, node names, handoff console prints, this run sheet, maps, and validator output from the participant. There are no in-world labels or objectives.
2. Record screen and microphone with consent, plus a separate timestamped observation sheet. Photograph/scan the two paper sketches with the session ID. The probe has no comprehension telemetry or restart key: **close and relaunch the scene for each participant** and confirm the H0 rear-pocket spawn. Do not use F5, which opens Awakening.
3. In the two pilots, verify that the H2 pocket can be identified without a participant-facing cue, that the final H0 fork offers an unambiguous choice of a path **toward the gallery ramp** (A) and a path that **stays on the ground** (B), and that nobody gets a clear triple-stack view before that fork. The handoff preserves the player's facing direction, so egocentric left/right is not a reliable instruction. If the ramp/ground distinction cannot be identified at the fork without orienting the player or revealing the proof, do not improvise a route cue mid-wave; record A/B comparison as untestable until the procedure is fixed.
4. Keep the A/B assignment concealed until after the H2 baseline sketch. The moderator must not gesture, move the mouse, point out a repair, or orient the player. At the exterior fork only, if the branch distinction passed the pilots and the player has not already seen the full proof, read one assigned navigation sentence: **A:** “For this run, take the path toward the ramp first; after that, explore as you like.” **B:** “For this run, take the path that stays on the ground first; after that, explore as you like.” This is route control, not a natural-choice observation. Record if the instruction could not be applied.
5. Fix a standard assistance rule before recruitment: allow exact control help if asked; after **two minutes without progress** in a receiver, ask only “What are you trying to do?” or “What have you tried?” If route help becomes necessary, use the same non-answer-bearing policy for everyone, timestamp and quote it, and mark route discovery as assisted. Never name the destination or tell them which cell works. Stop on motion sickness or withdrawal.

## 3. Verbatim moderator script and session sequence

| Point | Exact participant-facing wording | Moderator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Before launch** | “You will explore an unfinished first-person space. Play as you normally would. You may say what you notice or think is happening, but you do not have to talk constantly. I may ask you to pause at a few points to make a sketch. Take the time you need.” | Do not say “copies,” “three,” “facility state,” “proof,” or the H labels. If asked for a goal: “Explore whatever interests you.” |
| **Controls, only if requested** | “WASD moves; the mouse looks. Escape releases the cursor, and a left click recaptures it.” | No interaction key is needed in this probe. Log any help. |
| **H0, H1, H2 visits** | **No routine question.** If asked whether a theory is correct: “What makes you think that?” If stuck under the standard rule: “What are you trying to do?” | Note spontaneous accounts *before* any prompt. Entering each source receiver advances the visit automatically; never tell the player this in advance. Record receiver confusion and whether they look up at the QER support. |
| **H2, before entering its source receiver** | “Could you sketch your route so far and talk me through it?” Then, only after they finish: “What made you draw it this way?” and “If you returned to the first area, what would you expect to find?” | Ask the participant to stop moving; do not open an in-game pause or editor. Collect the **baseline** sketch and exact words. Distinguish a theory volunteered during play from one first produced by drawing. Do not confirm, paraphrase, or ask “Is it one place?” |
| **H0 return before an exterior triple view** | **No question.** | Timestamp any recognition or changed theory here. It is a *pre-view* insight, not success attributable to A or B. The arrival faces a partition; do not tell the participant to turn. |
| **First exterior approach** | **No routine question.** If five minutes pass after the first adequate view without an interpretation: “What do you make of what you have seen?” | Count five unassisted minutes from the first recorded adequate view, or stop earlier if the player is about to cross to the second approach. Record the actual first-view exposure duration; an early crossover shortens that opportunity and makes an A/B comparison inconclusive unless exposure is comparable. Keep spontaneous and neutral-elicited answers separate. Do not ask how many facilities there are. |
| **Before crossing to the other approach** | “Please sketch what you think you have seen. Talk me through it.” Then: “What led you to draw it this way?” | Lock the **first-view score** using this sketch, prior speech, and video. Do not suggest that their view *must* have changed. Do not praise or correct. |
| **After score lock** | “What else might explain what you saw?” “What would you expect if you retraced your route?” “What observation would help you decide?” | Let them visit the second viewpoint and answer these only after primary scoring. This is diagnostic corroboration, never a retroactive first-view pass. Debrief and reveal the test purpose only after all questions are complete. |

The H2 sketch can itself provoke spatial comparison. Preserve timestamps of **unprompted**, **sketch-elicited**, and **post-view** insights. A separate no-sketch pilot may estimate that reactivity, but silence without a sketch is not evidence of a particular baseline belief.

## 4. Observation log — record behavior before interpretation

| Phase | Timestamp/observable to capture |
| --- | --- |
| H0/H1/H2 | Arrival and exit time; actual view of twin seams / slanted splice / capped post; whether the player mentions or compares them; upward look at QER support; self-chosen backtracking; volunteered mental map and exact quote. |
| Receivers | Time searching; wrong/dead-end cell entries; whether the handoff feels like passage, teleport, loading, or reset; control help and route hints. The floor-level transfer is test scaffolding, so treat any interpretation it creates as a possible confound. |
| H2 baseline | Sketch/photo, exact explanation, prediction about returning, confidence in their own words, and whether the theory appeared *before* or *because of* the sketch. |
| H0 return | First recognition of paired seams; turn toward exterior; any correct inference before seeing the three stacks; any accidental early proof view. |
| Proof | Assigned route and **actual first revealing approach** (A/B/en route); time from H0 return to first view; first **adequate exposure**: at least two uninterrupted seconds with all three identity cues in the player camera and no full occlusion; total first-approach exposure before score lock; whether they apparently attend to the cues; lateral movement/parallax, comparison of upper/atrium/lower structures, revisits or predictions, exact first explanation and first revision. |
| Interventions | Verbatim moderator words, when spoken, who initiated them, any camera/route assistance, discomfort, technical issue, or prior spoiler. |

Code `opportunity visible` from the recording, independently of what the player says. If there is **no adequate view within eight minutes of H0 return**, log a navigation/attention outcome, not a comprehension failure. If a participant sees the three stacks before H2 baseline, keep the session as a *lead-in leakage* diagnostic but exclude it from the clean reversal denominator. If the full view occurs on an approach before A/B route instruction, record the actual exposure; do not relabel it as the assigned viewpoint. A/B attribution is inconclusive if this is common.

## 5. Score the mental model, not the noun

Code each session at four times: volunteered theory during visits (`T0`), H2 baseline after sketch (`T1`), H0-return insight before proof (`T2`), and locked first-view account (`T3`). The second view/debrief is `T4` diagnostic only. Two coders should score the recording and sketches independently; when possible, one coder should not know the assigned route. Resolve disagreement using quotes and frame timestamps, never moderator memory alone.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Baseline `B1`** | Participant-generated **one changing place**: successive atria are understood as one physical site in different conditions/configurations. They need not say “quantum.” Similar-looking rooms alone is not `B1`. |
| **Baseline `B2` / `B3` / `B4`** | Multiple fixed places / uncertain / another model (record verbatim, e.g. teleport to ordinary wings). Report all three categories; do not filter them out as “bad data.” |
| **Identity `I`** | None / some / **all three** earlier visit identities mapped to the current H0 and the two other visible stacks. A matching repair motif counts as identity evidence; a mere count does not. |
| **Coexistence `C`** | No / uncertain / **yes**: simultaneous, materially separated whole stacks, not one current atrium wearing three decorations or a screen. The player may call them “wings” if the physical model is right. |
| **Evidence `E`** | None / one type / **both** a remembered identity cue **and** a physical coexistence/topology cue (co-view, depth/parallax, fixed structural separation or vertical continuity). Two repair marks are still only one evidence type. |
| **Revision `R`** | No / tentative / evidence-backed rejection of their own `B1` map. A prediction about retracing the route can corroborate the account, but a post-debrief prediction cannot create an earlier pass. |

**Independent full reversal attributable to the proof view** = `B1` at the last pre-proof account (T1) **before answer-bearing help**, with no correction at T2, followed at T3 by `I=all three`, `C=yes`, `E=both`, and `R=evidence-backed`, before any explanation. Report whether `B1` was already volunteered at T0 or first appeared in the neutral T1 sketch; these are different strengths of evidence that the wrong model arose naturally. Record `S` if the correction was volunteered without a moderator interpretation question and `N` if produced only after the permitted neutral question/sketch. Both are player-generated, but report them separately. A correct model reached **during the T1 sketch or at T2** is a pre-proof discovery, not an A/B proof success. A `B2` participant with a correct T3 model has stable/early correct understanding, **not a reversal**.

**Partial** = correct count without a mapped history, incomplete mapping, coexistence recognized without rejecting the prior model, or an unsupported “three facilities” guess. **Failure after adequate view** = `B1` persists, identities are not connected to prior visits, or another model remains despite the available evidence. **Indeterminate/contaminated** = no adequate view, early proof leakage, technical break, prior spoiler, or answer-bearing help before T3. Report each reason separately. If the moderator supplies a repair, count, comparison, or “correct” confirmation, answers after that point are **moderator-influenced diagnostics**, not independent deduction. A neutral prompt may elicit reasoning; it must never supply the content of that reasoning.

## 6. Diagnose failure before choosing a revision

| Pattern in recordings/sketches | Primary interpretation to test | Revision target *after* this study, not during it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many players say “several places” before H2, or the H2 sketch first creates the idea | Intended wrong model was not naturally induced; cue difference, floor-level handoff, or the measurement prompt may be doing the work. | Revisit lead-in/measurement design; **do not** judge or revise the final proof view from these sessions. |
| Player had an adequate final view and counts three stacks, but cannot match H1/H2 to prior visits | Recognition/identity failure; inboard motifs echo rather than physically continue into the exterior surface. | Revise cue continuity, exposure, or framing if this repeats despite recorded cue opportunity. |
| Player correctly maps all three repairs yet says one facility is still in three states | Co-presence, scale, depth, or physical separation did not defeat the old model; possible transfer/load confound. | Compare actual first A/B views and parallax behavior before revising the viewpoint. |
| “Ordinary multi-wing building” | Could be correct fixed multiplicity in different vocabulary, or a failure to see three distinct QER–atrium–Awakening units. | Judge mapped prior visits and unit boundaries, not the noun “wing.” Do not revise solely because of terminology. |
| Repeated bridge/screen reading at A but not B, after comparable adequate exposure | Viewpoint A's geometry leaves a rival explanation alive. | Test B-first replication; consider revising A's proof framing only if identity mapping is already sound. |
| Stalls in receivers or does not turn at H0 return | Navigation prevented a clean reveal; route assistance may contaminate timing. | Fix navigation/readability before interpreting G2. |

## 7. Decision gate after the first wave

Report raw counts for **all recruited**, **eligible**, `B1`/`B2`/`B3`/`B4`, adequate/indeterminate, T2 early insights, and T3 full (`S` versus `N`), partial, and failure. Split clean first-view outcomes by **assigned route** and **actual first adequate view**. Quote at least one confirming and one disconfirming case; do not substitute a compelling anecdote for the denominators.

- **Insufficient to judge reversal:** fewer than six unspoiled `B1` participants obtain an adequate first view, or widespread early leakage/route hints contaminate the exposure. Recruit further with the same procedure or diagnose why G1 fails. Do not claim the proof view succeeded or failed.
- **Provisionally keep the current geometry for another blind round:** with at least six clean `B1` cases, roughly two-thirds or more reach **independent evidence-backed full reversal** before explanation, including cases from both actual first-view approaches with comparable exposure, and no repeated navigation/identity/depth failure dominates. Check the T0-versus-T1 split: if the wrong model appears only after the sketch, do not claim the lead-in naturally induced it. This is a pragmatic triage threshold, **not** population validation or permission to call the mystery solved.
- **Revise identity evidence first:** repeated clean cases (for example, three or more) see simultaneous structures but cannot map them to the earlier repair histories despite a recorded opportunity to inspect those repairs. Preserve the proof view while testing cue continuity.
- **Revise a proof viewpoint first:** repeated clean cases map prior identities yet reject or miss simultaneous fixed separation, especially if one actual first-view approach produces bridge/screen/state readings and the other does not. Require adequate, comparable exposure; en-route/crossover contamination makes an A/B verdict **inconclusive**.
- **If mixed or borderline:** retain the build, report the competing accounts, and run another blind wave before choosing a geometric change. The lead-in and view can fail independently.
