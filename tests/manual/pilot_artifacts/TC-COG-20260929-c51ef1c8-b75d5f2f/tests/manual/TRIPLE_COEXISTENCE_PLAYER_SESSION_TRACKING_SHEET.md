# Triple Coexistence — Player Session Tracking Sheet

**Copy once per participant. Staff-facing only; do not show this sheet or its categories to the player.** Read prompts from the [blind playtest protocol](TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_BLIND_PLAYTEST.md), not from this form. Use the [pilot checklist](TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_COGNITIVE_PILOT_CHECKLIST.md) before scored sessions and the [result-analysis template](../../docs/world/TRIPLE_COEXISTENCE_PLAYTEST_RESULT_ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md) for later adjudication. During play, prioritize timestamps and verbatim words; complete interpretation from the recording afterward. Record what the player **saw, said, sketched, and predicted** before assigning a model. Human comprehension is **UNVALIDATED** until actual unbriefed sessions are analyzed.

## 5. Mental-model classification — fill **after** the `T3` record is locked

Choose the closest observed trajectory, not the best-sounding phrase. These descriptive categories do **not** replace the protocol's full/partial/failure criteria. Add a secondary note if the player changes model more than once.

- [ ] **A — One-changing-facility model formed and revised:** `B1` persisted through `T1` and `T2`; after adequate first view the player used earlier place identities **and** simultaneous physical evidence to revise it. Note whether the result meets full `I/C/E/R` criteria: ______.
- [ ] **B — Multiple fixed facilities inferred early:** player reached this model during visits, at the H2 sketch, or on H0 return, before the first exterior proof view. Exact time and basis: ______.
- [ ] **C — Multiple spaces read as ordinary architecture:** player saw several spaces but did not map the prior visits to distinct persistent stacks. If “wings” was merely their name for correctly mapped units, do **not** use C on vocabulary alone. Missing relationship: ______.
- [ ] **D — Quantum-state explanation maintained:** after adequate view, player still treated the successive atria as one physical place replacing/changing state. What physical evidence did they see, and why did their model survive? ______.
- [ ] **E — No stable explanation:** uncertainty or conflicting maps remain at `T3`. Record alternatives rather than forcing one: ______.
- [ ] **X — Other, partial, or unscorable:** e.g. correct-count guess without history, only two visits mapped, no adequate view, early leak, moderator influence, recording fault. Specify: ______.

Protocol component codes: `I` prior identities mapped ☐ none ☐ some ☐ all three; `C` coexisting separated stacks ☐ no ☐ uncertain ☐ yes; `E` evidence ☐ none ☐ one type ☐ both identity and topology; `R` revision ☐ no ☐ tentative ☐ evidence-backed. Evidence timestamps/quotes: ______.

Provisional session outcome: ☐ full proof-view reversal ☐ partial ☐ failure **after adequate view** ☐ early correct / no reversal opportunity ☐ indeterminate/contaminated. Reason and affected measure: ______. A second coder should verify this later from the recording and sketches; do not decide it from a memorable quote alone.

Do not answer “Did the player discover three facilities?” as a stand-alone pass/fail question. Report **how the explanation changed, when, and on which evidence**. The existing technical validation means the probe functions; this sheet does not establish that people experience the intended cognitive reversal. No single session authorizes a geometry or puzzle revision.
